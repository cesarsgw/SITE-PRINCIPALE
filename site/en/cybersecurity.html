Our approach

1

Mapping & audit

Assessment of your exposure (systems, network, applications) and prioritization of vulnerabilities based on their real-world criticality.

2

Implementation of recommendations: network segmentation, access management, security policies and a prioritized remediation plan.

3

FAQ

How long does a PASSI audit take?

It depends on the scope: from a few days for a targeted penetration test to several weeks for a full architecture audit. An initial scoping call lets us estimate the duration precisely.

Does the managed SOC require dedicated hardware?

No. We rely on lightweight agents and connectors compatible with your existing infrastructure, with no service disruption during setup.

Are we affected by NIS2?

It depends on your industry and size. We run a quick eligibility assessment before starting any compliance work.
