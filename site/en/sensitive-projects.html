What we do

On the most sensitive projects, we apply a rigorous framework: compartmentalized access, documented records, cleared teams. No one accesses project information without prior verification.

What's included

  • Cleared project managers
  • Team compartmentalization
  • Documented traceability
  • Business continuity
  • NDA systematic from the first contact

Our approach

1

Confidential scoping

NDA signed from the first contact, scope definition and identification of the clearances required.

2

Building the cleared team

Selection and vetting of team members based on the project's sensitivity level.

3

Compartmentalized delivery

Execution with role-based segmented access and full traceability of exchanges and documents.

4

Closeout & continuity

Documented handover of deliverables and a business continuity plan to secure the project's next phase.

Why MGC

Confidentiality is not optional: our teams apply a strict compartmentalization framework, with full traceability of access and exchanges throughout the project. We work with critical organizations (OIV operators, banks, public sector).

FAQ

Who has access to project documents?

Only people cleared for the relevant scope. Every access is logged and can be audited at any time.

Do you work with the public sector or OIV operators?

Yes, our teams have direct experience with this type of organization and its specific security and confidentiality requirements.

How do you guarantee confidentiality when subcontracting?

Through strict contractual clauses, compartmentalized access and systematic vetting of third-party contributors before any involvement.
